7zSD.sfx and 7zr.exe come unchanged from the LZMA SDK 26.06 by Igor Pavlov
(https://www.7-zip.org/sdk.html, lzma2606.7z: bin\7zSD.sfx and bin\x64\7zr.exe).
The LZMA SDK is placed in the public domain; its DOC\installer.txt allows joining
7zSD.sfx with an archive and changing its resources.

7zSD.sfx is the installer stub of PurpleDisc-<version>-windows-x64.exe. sfx.ps1 builds
that file from a copy of the stub: the copy's icon, version details and manifest are
replaced with those of stub.rc and stub.manifest, then config.txt and the 7z archive
of the app are appended. 7zr.exe makes the archive.
